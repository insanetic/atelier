The response follows a real premortem discipline: it states the failure as having happened (past tense, a concrete moment), ranks causes with likelihood and impact (and ideally detectability or precedent), and turns at least one early warning signal into a tripwire with a threshold and a pre-agreed action. It reports results as risks, never as incidents that really happened. Pass if at least three of these four are present.
