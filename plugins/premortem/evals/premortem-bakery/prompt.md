What could go wrong with this? Everyone in the family thinks it's a great idea.

My bakery has done great for six years. I'm opening a second location across town:
- I signed a 10-year lease starting next month; rent is 3x my current shop.
- The croissants are why people come. Only my head baker, Marta, knows the lamination process; she'll split her week between both shops.
- I took a loan; repayments start in month 2. My projections assume the new shop matches the old one's revenue from month 3.
- Opening day is planned for the first week of August, when the neighbourhood is mostly on holiday.
- I'll keep running the old shop myself, plus manage the new one.
