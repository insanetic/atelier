The response reasons about the capability itself rather than the intended use, and covers at least FOUR of:
1. Concrete misuse by named kinds of holders (e.g. scammers doing "grandparent"/CEO-fraud calls, harassment or non-consensual impersonation, political disinformation, bypassing voice authentication).
2. Irreversibility: open weights cannot be recalled once released.
3. Uplift: how much this adds over what already exists (3 seconds, real time, consumer GPU), rather than assuming "someone else will release it anyway" settles it.
4. The race rationalization (competitor pressure) named and examined.
5. Controls or alternatives: staged or gated release, API with monitoring, watermarking or detection tools released alongside, consent verification, releasing a weaker version, publishing detection first.
6. Who else should be involved before the decision (external review, safety/ethics board, affected parties, authentication vendors).
