An FAQ (or separate articles) is fine. If an FAQ is written or drafted, each answer is at most three sentences and contains no numbered procedure; a question that would need several steps gets its own article (written or proposed) that the answer points to. Links only to articles that exist or are written in the same response; a missing one is named, not linked. Asking for facts the request does not give (how to reach support, where the period is chosen) instead of inventing them is fine.
