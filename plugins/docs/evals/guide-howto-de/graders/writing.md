The reader is addressed with 'Sie' throughout and never with 'du'/'dein'. Steps are a numbered list with one action each (opening the menu, choosing the period and selecting the export button are not crammed into one step), the labels and menu paths the notes give appear in bold without quotes (e.g. **Einstellungen** > **Abrechnung** or **Einstellungen > Abrechnung**, **CSV exportieren**); a control the notes do not name may stay unnamed, and the email arriving after a few minutes is stated as the result of the export step. No 'einfach', 'lediglich' or 'natürlich'. Judge the article file only, not how the assistant talks to the user in the chat.
