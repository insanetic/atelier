Specific factual claims are traceable to sources: retention windows, status codes, header names, what a provider does. Traceable means each such claim sits next to a link, a file:line, or a citation id that resolves to a listed source. Claims about our code point at the file. Pass if nearly every specific claim about another system has a source attached. Fail if specifics are stated without sources, or if any claim looks invented or contradicts well-known facts. For example, Stripe documents a 24-hour key retention, and the IETF draft names the `Idempotency-Key` header.
